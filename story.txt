The lights in the old lighthouse had been dark for thirty years, so when Maya saw a beam sweep across the bay at midnight, she knew something was wrong. She grabbed her flashlight and ran down the rocky path, her heart pounding louder than the waves. At the top of the spiral stairs, the lamp room door stood wide open, and a single brass key sat glowing on the floor. Then, from somewhere below, she heard footsteps climbing toward her.

Maya gripped the brass key and pressed herself against the wall as a tall figure stepped into the lamp room. It was her grandfather, a man everyone in town believed had vanished at sea three decades ago. "I'm sorry I took so long to come home," he whispered, his coat still dripping with seawater. Together they slid the key into the great lamp, and the beam roared to life, sweeping across the bay. Far out on the water, a lost fleet of fishing boats turned toward the light and finally began to sail home.
